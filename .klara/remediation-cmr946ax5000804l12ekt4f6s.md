## Issue Description
Ensure <img> elements have alternative text or a role of none or presentation

## Affected Element
Selector: `img[src$="g2.svg"]`

## User Impact
Screen reader users cannot understand the purpose or content of the image, creating a barrier to accessing information. Decorative images without proper markup cause screen readers to announce unclear filenames instead of being skipped.

## BFSG/Legal Risk
Violates WCAG 1.1.1 Level A (critical), exposing the organization to legal liability under accessibility legislation (ADA, EN 301 549, AODA).

## AI-Suggested Fix

### Before
```html
<img src="g2.svg" />
```

### After
```html
<img src="g2.svg" alt="" role="presentation" />
```

**Confidence:** 85%
