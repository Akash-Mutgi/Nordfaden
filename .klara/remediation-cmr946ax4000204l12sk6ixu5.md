## Issue Description
Ensure <img> elements have alternative text or a role of none or presentation

## Affected Element

## User Impact
Screen reader users cannot understand the purpose or content of the hero image, resulting in a gap in their ability to comprehend the page's primary visual message. This creates a frustrating and incomplete experience for users relying on assistive technology.

## BFSG/Legal Risk
