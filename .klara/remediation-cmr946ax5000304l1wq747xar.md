## Issue Description
Ensure <img> elements have alternative text or a role of none or presentation

## Affected Element
Selector: `img[src$="p1.svg"]`

## User Impact
Screen reader users cannot understand the purpose or content of the image, making the page less usable and potentially causing them to miss important information.

## BFSG/Legal Risk
Violates WCAG 1.1.1 Level A, creating legal exposure under accessibility legislation (ADA, AODA, EN 301 549, etc.).

### After
